# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Protect personal data and respect source owners.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and bypassing security or access controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
